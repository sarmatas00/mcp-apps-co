# Top 10 Data Visualization MCP Apps for 2026

Data visualization is one of the most popular use cases for MCP Apps. Instead of describing your data, why not render an interactive chart that your AI can help you explore? Here are the top 10 data visualization MCP Apps available today.

- Export to PNG/SVG
- Dark mode support

## How to Choose the Right App

Consider these factors:

1. **Your AI Client** — Not all apps work everywhere
2. **Data Source** — Does it connect to your data?
3. **Interactivity Level** — View-only vs. fully interactive
4. **Export Options** — Can you save or share the visualization?
